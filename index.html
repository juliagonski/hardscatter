The approach

Reinforce reasoning. Then let it work.

We reward the process, not just the answer — so the model learns to show its work and get it right for the right reasons.

01

Pose the problem

Hand it a question — physical, mathematical, or messy and real. It scopes assumptions before touching the math.

02

Reason, don't guess

Step-by-step derivation with self-verification baked in at every junction. Dead ends get backtracked, not hidden.

03

Prove it out

Dimensional checks, limiting cases, and adversarial review before anything is called a result.
